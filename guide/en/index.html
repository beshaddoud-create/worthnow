What is WorthNow?

WorthNow is a ledger and calculator for gold shops and money changers, and for anyone who owns assets and wants to know what they are worth. You record what you own — currencies, gold, silver, crypto — and everything that happens to it: sales, purchases, exchanges, expenses and debts. So at any moment you know:

What do I own?
What is it worth?
How did it change?
And why?

It suits the gold-shop owner, the money changer, anyone who trades several currencies, and anyone who wants to know their wealth after every move.

Three things to know
  • Works offline: all the arithmetic happens on your phone; the internet is only for live prices and your subscription.
  • Your data stays with you: your ledger is never uploaded to any server.
  • You set the value: you write the valuation prices yourself; live prices are only a guide.

Before you start

Three things to keep in mind

Important: The valuation currency is fixed by your first balance or operation, so choose it carefully. To see your figures in another currency, use the “display currency” (section 2).
Important: Nothing before today is accounted for. If you bought your gold years ago for less, that difference stays outside the app — on purpose: you start from today’s value.
Tip: You write the valuation price. The app shows live gold and silver prices as a guide, and writes none of them without your tap (section 5).
Tip: Everything you entered in these steps can be changed later from “Records”.

Two currencies

One to value in, one to view in

For the books
Valuation currency

The yardstick of your books: everything you record is computed in it. You choose it on first launch, and it is fixed by your first operation, because changing it would falsify the figures.

For viewing
Display currency

Shows your figures in another currency, for viewing only: dollars, gold, any asset with a valuation price. Change it whenever you like — no saved figure changes.

Pricing the operation

Spread and Commission

Spread200 × 138 = 27,600

You sell 200 dollars. Two prices, as you post them in your shop: buy at 136.5, sell at 138. The app picks by itself: what comes in at the buy price, what goes out at the sell price. Here the dollar went out, so the sell price was used.

Commission with a fee12.5 × (16,000 + 500) = 206,250

One price plus a fee. For gold it is the “Per-gram fee” (the making charge), added to the price; other metals have a per-kilo or per-carat fee.

Pricing the operation

Manual, and a percentage

Manual24,500 ÷ 1.5 = 16,333

You type Out (1.5 grams) and In (24,500) yourself, as agreed for a lump sum; the app shows the “Resulting price” for reading only.

Commission as %100 × 1 + 1% = 101

For currencies the commission is a percentage: exchanging 100 USDT at 1 with 1% gives 101 USD.

Tip: In Spread, Commission and Live you type one quantity and the app works out the other; in Manual you type both. The method is kept for display and for filtering Records; the arithmetic runs on the two quantities.

How are they worked out?

Gram price = ounce price ÷ 31.1035 × karat ÷ 24 × dollar price

That is the buy price; add the sell margin and you have the sell price. Silver works the same with its fineness: 925 of 1000.

Gold ounce4,165.20 $
One gram of 21K117.18 $
At a dollar of 137 · buy16,053 SYP
With a 1.28% margin · sell16,258 SYP
Important: These prices are a guide, at your dollar rate and margin; they can lag or be wrong. Nothing reaches your book without your tap: Use for valuation, or Live in the calculator.
Tip: When the world market is closed or its sources are down, the app takes the gold price from PAXG — a crypto token where each unit is an ounce of gold, traded around the clock — and says so in a line above the bulletin.

The app asks for prices only while you look: about every 15 seconds on the Prices screen and once a minute in the calculator — nothing in the background. Offline it says “Couldn’t fetch prices”, and the app keeps working.

AdvancesNew

Paid today, collected in a few days

A customer pays for a set being made for her and collects it next week: record the sale as “You owe”. The money is recorded as an advance and the gold stays in your vault until delivery.

  1. 1
    Awaiting delivery What was paid and not handed over: the piece, its weight and day.
  2. 2
    Delivered On delivery day it completes the advance, and the profit counts that day.
Tip: A purchase works the other way: you paid a trader who delivers later — record it as “Owes you” and complete it with Received.
Contents

Collecting and paying

From “Action” in the customer file

Collect

A payment you received. No line moves — the profit was made on the day of the sale, not of payment.

Pay

A payment you made. No line moves.

Settle

What couldn’t be collected, or you waived. It lands in the “Debt settlement” line and cancels no earlier profit.

Old debt

A debt from before the app, or one you forgot, recorded now. It goes into capital, not into a line.

Tip: A payment can be cash or an asset: a customer may settle in gold or dollars.

How to protect your data

Export

Creates a file to keep or send. A backup carries your whole ledger — give it only to someone you trust.

Import

Replaces everything in the app with the file: no partial restore, no merging. A damaged file is refused and nothing changes.

“Before last restore”

After every restore the app keeps your ledger as it was, so you can go back if you restored the wrong file.

Important: If the phone is lost or the app deleted without a backup, the data is gone and nobody can bring it back. Save a copy after every working day, and keep one off the phone.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for everyday situations

Frequently asked questions

Calculator and pricing

Q: How do I enter an exchange?

Tap one of the two fields and type the quantity, then the price in the price card; the other field fills in. The coin opens the asset list and ⇅ reverses the direction.

Example: You sell a customer 200 dollars at 138: type 200 in Out and the price, and 27,600 appears in In.

Q: Spread, Commission, Manual or Live — what’s the difference?

Spread: buy and sell prices as you post them. Commission: one price plus a fee or percentage. Manual: you type both fields for a lump sum. Live: market prices for gold and silver.

Example: Dollar mid price 137 with 1%: you sell at 138.37 and buy at 135.63.

Q: I made a mistake — what now?

Tap it in Records, then Edit or Delete; the app recalculates what follows. For a forgotten one, use “Save with an earlier date”.

Example: Forgot one from yesterday: enter it, then ⋮ → “Save with an earlier date” and pick yesterday.

Q: Do live prices go into my book by themselves?

No. They are a guide; nothing is written without your tap: Use for valuation, or Live in the calculator.

Frequently asked questions

Gold, vault and figures

Q: How do I handle gold and its karats?

Gold is one entry in the lists, but its karats (24, 22, 21, 18) are separate assets, so a gram of one is never added to a gram of another.

Example: You sold 10 grams of 21K: choose gold, then “21”, and type 10 in Out.

Q: What is the valuation price, and why doesn’t it update itself?

It is the price you go by for each asset today, and what you own is measured with it. You update it in Vault → Update prices, or Prices → Use for valuation.

Example: You bought dollars at 136 and the market moved to 137: update the valuation price and your worth rises without selling.

Q: When does profit appear, and what is the valuation difference?

Realised profit shows in “Trading result” whenever something other than your currency leaves the vault. A rise in an asset you still hold shows in “Valuation difference” — profit not yet realised.

Example: You bought 100 dollars for 13,600 and sold them for 13,800: a trading result of 200.

Q: The count shows a difference — what do I do?

Type what you actually found, not the difference; the app works it out and records it as “Count difference”.

Example: The book says 100 dollars and you counted 98: type 98, and a two-dollar shortfall shows.

Security and other situations

Q: How do I protect my data?

Your data lives only on your phone. Save a copy from ☰ → Backup somewhere off the phone; you can restore it on any phone.

Example: One habit is enough: a backup at the end of every working day.

Q: Why can’t I take a screenshot of the app?

Because “Hide in recent apps” is on with the lock. Turn it off in Settings → Security; the lock stays.

Q: Can I change the valuation currency later?

No — it is fixed by the first operation, because changing it would falsify past figures. To see figures in another currency, use the display currency.

Q: Why does the app refuse to delete an operation?

Because some balance would go negative at a later moment. Fix what follows first, or edit it instead.

Q: I moved to a new phone — what about my subscription?

Message us from “Subscription & licence” with the new device code and we’ll move it; restore your data from a backup taken on the old phone.
