Privacy Policy

Your books stay on your phone. Below is everything that leaves it, line by line, and why.

Updated 26 October 2026WorthNow for Android

At a glance

Your ledger stays on your phone

Trades, balances, customers, prices, invoices and your shop details are saved only on your device.

No account, no ads, no tracking

No sign-up and no password. No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code inside the app.

Two connections, both to us

The app talks only to our own server: to check your subscription, and to fetch gold and silver prices when you open them.

Nothing sold or shared

We don’t sell your data or share it with anyone.

What we keep

On our server, so your trial and subscription work:

Device

Device code (TU-XXXX-XXXX), the scrambled device ID, phone model, Android and app versions, language, first and last check-in, the number of check-ins, and a short label we may add to recognise it.

Trial

Its start and end dates, the days you used the app, and the ledger fingerprint.

SubscriptionOnly if you subscribe

Plan, price, dates and status, the name and phone number you contacted us with, and any note we add.

History

A log of changes to your subscription, such as activation, renewal and suspension, so any mistake can be traced and fixed.

Internet address

Not stored. We keep only a salted, scrambled trace of it from your first check-in, to stop the server being flooded.

Where: Supabase, in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Like every hosting service, Supabase keeps short-lived technical logs of requests, which include the internet address, currently for about one day. We don’t copy them into our records.

Never collected

  • Amounts and balances
  • Customer names and phone numbers
  • Invoices and notes
  • Your shop details and logo
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Location
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Text messages
  • Notifications
  • Advertising ID

Your invoice logo is picked with Android’s photo picker: the app receives only that one image, and it stays on your phone.

Permissions

Internet
The subscription check and live prices.
Vibration
A light tap when you save or switch.
Fingerprint or face unlock
The optional app lock. Android checks it; the app only learns whether it worked.
Screen-capture detection (Android 14+)
Needed by the screen protection behind the optional “Hide in recent apps” setting. The app keeps no screenshots.
Storage (Android 12 and older)
Saving backups and exports where you choose.

Blocked on purpose: camera, microphone, photo library and drawing over other apps. A system library also declares the install-referrer service; the app doesn’t use it.

Your choices

Your data, your files

Back up or erase everything from the app at any time. A backup holds your whole ledger, so share it only with someone you trust. Uninstalling the app removes the ledger from your phone; we hold no copy, so only your backup can bring it back.

Ask us

Ask what we hold about your device, or ask us to delete it. We delete the device record, its history and any ended subscription, and keep only its trial record so the free trial can’t restart. We can’t delete a device while a subscription is active on it, or while it is blocked for misuse.

Messaging us

If you message us on WhatsApp from the app, the message is filled in with your device code and the plan you chose. You see it before you send it.

Working offline

The app keeps working without internet: up to 7 days during the trial, 60 days on a subscription with an end date, and with no limit on a lifetime plan. Then it needs one connection.

Security

  • Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Our server signs each answer and the app checks the signature, so a forged answer can’t change your subscription.
  • Device IDs and internet addresses are only ever kept scrambled.
  • You can lock the app with your phone’s fingerprint, face or PIN, and hide it in recent apps.

Children

WorthNow is a tool for shops and traders. It isn’t meant for children.

Changes

This text is the same in the app and on the web. If anything changes, we update both, with the date.
